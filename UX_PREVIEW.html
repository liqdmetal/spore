#crew · priv 🔒
— channels ride a relay: public readable live, private sealed to a channel key, all TTL-rot.
— whisper (1:1): a short line in the tx payload, encrypted to your wallet, delivered via your own node — no relay, no box.
— long nobody-but-us: body encrypted to your key, held by the sender, fetched peer-to-peer when you're both online, then rot + key erased. The 95-byte whisper is only the pointer.
— @names resolve through DERO Name Service (deroname → address) before send.
This is a UX preview, not the live TELA doc. The engines behind it are built and mainnet-verified (whisper send/recv, long nobody-but-us end-to-end, mycelium-peer transport, deroname resolution). Making this exact surface render as a TELA on-chain contract — the app living in chain state, no app server — is the wiring left. The key-exchange (share a mycelium pubkey + reachable peer address) is currently copy-paste and is the main rough edge to smooth.
