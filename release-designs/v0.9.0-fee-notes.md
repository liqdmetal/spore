## Worked example — what deployment day looks like with real numbers

Fill order inside Patch 1a (all from the second `spore contract estimate`
run, the one with `-mailbox`):

1. `<<EST-GAS-PRICE>>` — the `gas price:` row (wei, decimal, as printed).
2. `<<EST-DEPLOY-GAS>>` / `<<EST-DELIVER-GAS>>` / `<<EST-BURN-GAS>>` — the
   three per-op gas numbers (comma-formatted as the command prints them).
   If burn printed the fallback line, keep "the labeled conservative
   fallback" wording and consider re-running against a slot that exists —
   a live burn estimate beats the constant.
3. The four ETH costs (`<<EST-DEPLOY-ETH>>`, `<<EST-DELIVER-ETH>>`,
   `<<EST-BURN-ETH>>`, `<<EST-ROUND-ETH>>`) — the right-hand column of each
   row plus the `deliver+burn` row, exactly as `FormatWei` prints them.
4. `<<EST-N-ROUNDS>>` — the `-rounds` value used (default 12).
5. `<<EST-FUNDING-TOTAL>>` — the `funding total:` line's final figure.
6. `<<EST-DATE>>` — the date the estimate ran (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Sanity: the total should be cents-scale at 2026 Base fee levels. If it
   is not, stop — something is wrong (wrong chain, wrong RPC, gas spike) —
   and re-measure rather than publishing a surprising number.

The stub fill-ins (`<<EST-STUB-*>>`) come from the latest green
`sepolia rehearsal smoke (stub chain)` CI log, never from a local re-run
gone stale. Today's stub constants, for recognizing a drifted log: 1 gwei
gas price, 250k deploy / 62.5k deliver / 35k burn (fallback), ≈0.00142 ETH
for 12 rounds.
