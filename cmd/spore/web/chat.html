Two ways to talk. They have different privacy properties — pick deliberately.

◆ Rooms this box

Public room — anyone who can reach this box and guess the name reads it. Fast, no wallet needed, kept in memory/disk and rots on a TTL.

Private room — every line is AES-GCM encrypted in your browser. The box stores ciphertext only. Share the key out-of-band; whoever lacks it sees 🔒 unreadable line.

◇ E2 message forward-private, 1:1

X3DH + Double Ratchet. Every message ratchets its keys — a recorded ciphertext cannot be decrypted with keys compromised later, and a stolen device heals after the next exchange. No relay, no box in the middle.

Bodies ride your own mailbox; only an opaque pointer rides the chain. First contact needs the recipient's prekey and pinned sig, saved once with spore msg mail add.

Legacy whispers (stateless X25519) are receive-only: they are not forward-private, so new sends are refused.
